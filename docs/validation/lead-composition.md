# Lead simplification and method composition

This revision consolidates repeated lead instructions and makes the integration of relevant methods explicit. The routing map and method bodies stay unchanged. The [decision and preservation map](../decisions/2026-09-24-lead-composition.md) describe the intended behavior. The evidence supports a maintenance change with useful sampled outcomes; it does not establish improved first discovery or resolution of the broader routing concern.

## Comparison and source coverage

Ten preselected native Codex attempts compared Atlas 0.9.1 with the initial revision: one sample per condition for each task below, using Astra at medium effort and the same fixtures, evaluator, permissions and 240-second total bound. The two new composition cases were prepared by another agent before it read candidate instructions. Their [rubric](../../evals/composition-review.md) stayed outside trial projects. Reviews inspected responses, actual tool actions and resulting artifacts. Reviewers did not author the candidate, but version identities were visible; the composition reviewer authored its rubric. This is not a blinded or broad held-out study.

The primary candidate contained the restored worker-dispatch notice and a 2,095-word lead, versus 3,205 words on main. All five candidate trials and four baseline trials completed. The remaining baseline reached its bound during the second turn; it was retained and not rerun. Native completion is separate from the semantic judgments below.

Retained qualifications matter. The onboarding candidate consulted more distinct references than the baseline despite a shorter answer, and both produced elaborate planning notes. The primary recovery candidate's final close put owner choices before missing-source inspection, while its brief had the better sequence. The search completion difference is one observation under a bound, not a causal speed or reliability result. A successful baseline prevents attributing its already-demonstrated behavior to this revision.

## Review corrections and final candidate

Independent source review compared the baseline obligations and referenced procedures. A separate Copilot Sol/high review caught the omitted upfront worker-assignment notice; it was restored and independently rechecked before all primary trials.

Opus 5.5 at extra-high effort challenged the architecture and implementation. Three subsequent clarifications distinguish required independence from optional delegation, preserve method sources and implications needed for unfinished work without a consultation ledger, and limit the workflow-machinery warning to procedure failures. A fresh source review found no concrete regression in those corrections. The final lead is 2,126 words, about 34% shorter than main; that is a maintenance property, not a measured context or quality improvement.

One separate recovery diagnostic exercises the final source after those clarifications. Its two native turns completed within the same bound. Independent review confirmed preserved mission, authority, unfinished constraints and source pointers without a consultation ledger. Its final recommendation correctly sequences missing-source inspection before remaining choices. The other four primary case results belong to the preceding candidate; they were not silently reassigned or rerun. Delegation and procedure-policy clarifications have source review only.

## Checks and limits

The evaluator's 39 correctness tests pass. Package versions are aligned at 0.9.2, the lead routing table is unchanged, and local link/whitespace checks cover the changed guidance. These checks do not establish model adherence.

All primary tool captures parsed without errors. Some bundled guidance outputs were truncated in the onboarding, search and design cases in both conditions. These warnings occur in the native tool-call outputs, not merely the review packet's display clipping: affected reads returned partial guidance. Reviewers checked actual returned sections and subsequent rereads; requested paths do not prove every byte reached the model. No causal effect of truncation was established. Retained snapshots corroborate planning-only changes, and captured actions showed no prohibited operation; this is not proof of every transient or external effect.

The primary cases exercise useful composition, changing evidence, visible choices, advice-only boundaries, the lead's use notices, closing guidance, selective restraint and artifact recovery. Worker dispatch/context transfer and use reporting, Arena/Reflect gating, unavailable guidance, prohibited continuity writes, changed-authority persistence, cumulative repair limits, long-session direction checks and continuation of implementation remain source-preserved only in this round. Recovery explicitly reselects Atlas in a fresh thread with project artifacts; it does not test automatic restoration or actual compaction.

The native host was Codex CLI/app-server 0.153.4. No native Copilot workplace trial, packaging comparison or second execution model was run here. Copilot reviews used supplied text and are not host-compatibility tests. Exact source identities, prompts, all eleven attempts, corrections and detailed reviews remain in local maintainer evidence. The installed release was not changed to the unmerged candidate.
