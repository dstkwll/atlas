## Arena brief alignment

The `arena-revisions` and `arena-no-qualifier` cases use synthetic returned designs and the [Arena review criteria](../../evals/review.md#arena-brief-alignment). Native Copilot profile checks compared main with the revised runbook under matching conditions, alongside `arena-confirmation`.

The final candidate selected a compliant design over a higher-scoring alternative, rejected a later revision that lost rejected rows, retained compatible interface improvements, and applied an explicit retention amendment narrowly. When no finalist met the required evidence, it recorded no qualifying winner and kept its repair recommendation provisional in both the artifact and final response. Acknowledgment did not launch an Arena. Successful source reads preceded the comparisons; observed writes stayed within planning artifacts.

Main also handled the successive revisions correctly. Its all-ineligible comparison preserved the requirements but named a final winner with mandatory repairs outstanding; the final candidate avoided that misleading status. These small observations do not establish a reliable improvement rate.

Native checks revealed each revision only at its follow-up turn. The reusable Codex runner makes the full fixture available upfront, so an equivalent delayed-discovery claim requires checking actual read timing. Real competitors, tournament supervision, final-review reading order, compaction and workplace reliability remain unverified. The final confirmation proposal omitted an overall deadline, review allowance and the project delegation restriction from its future launch conditions; no launch occurred, and delegation tools were unavailable. Fixture diagnostics and intermediate failures remain in local maintainer evidence.

## Packaging and verification limits

The documentation command was discoverable. Argument-hint presentation and cross-host invocation controls remain unverified. Structural checks resolve product links and parse metadata; they do not establish host compatibility or agent judgment.

The evaluator's unit tests check evaluation infrastructure, not skill adherence. Handling unavailable HTML recovery validation has source review but no behavioral verification. See [turn-ending guidance checks](turn-ending-guidance.md) for conversational continuation.
