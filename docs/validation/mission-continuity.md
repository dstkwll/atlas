# Mission continuity and bounded investigations

## Change and retained behavior

Atlas now explicitly preserves the parent mission, major milestones, current position, consequential unknowns and investigation return point in existing project records. Discovery should expose a route into implementation and justify a separate probe. Sufficient evidence ends a probe; reaching its bound pauses work for reassessment. Explicit limits remain binding, while a self-selected estimate can be revised visibly only with new evidence within authority. Verification follows the artifact's intended claim rather than automatically hardening disposable code.

The change preserves collaborative consequential design, proactive investigation, ordinary implementation freedom and actual security/privacy obligations. It adds no runtime, mandatory tracker, phase sequence or duplicate record. The charter reflects the accepted clarification; package versions advance together to 0.7.4.

## Evaluation design

Four synthetic cases were selected before inference, with expected decisions in `evals/review.md`. Main and candidate use the same evaluator, fixtures, native Codex host, Sol model at low effort, and a 150-second limit per case. Each completed condition has one sample per case. The recovery case uses two turns separated by a fresh native thread: it retains project artifacts and reselects the local Atlas skill without copying conversation messages.

These are planning recommendations and artifact-recovery checks. No device probe, production implementation, monetary cap, long autonomous loop or actual compaction is exercised. The original brief remains accessible during recovery. Source assertions and completed host calls are not behavioral passes.

## Observations

Results are recorded after manual inspection of emitted responses and saved planning artifacts. Automatic facts establish preserved guidance and planning-only changes, not semantic correctness.

Candidate observations:

- **Direct implementation route:** selected import/preview as the first useful slice, retained later milestones, and did not impose a separate PoC. Proposed CLI/filter details remained distinguishable from accepted intent.
- **Warranted probe:** identified frame decoding as choice-changing uncertainty and retained supported/unsupported/inconclusive outcomes. However, it called the probe bounded without supplying a practical time/work limit and proposed improving capture once after inconclusive evidence. This is a partial miss, not a passing demonstration of bounded investigation.
- **Recovery after success:** saved and recovered the parent mission and milestones, treated the probe as complete, and rejected unrelated stress/concurrency/retry work. The fresh lead also treated absent existing application source as a blocker to a credible implementation plan; this is an overcautious planning limitation for a potentially new project.
- **Exhausted inconclusive setup:** preserved the two consumed attempts, distinguished setup failure from evidence about decoding, rejected custom runtime/benchmark expansion, and recommended the ordinary parser with remaining product decisions exposed.

Baseline observations:

- **Direct implementation route:** also selected import/preview without a separate PoC and preserved later milestones.
- **Warranted probe:** identified decoding uncertainty and explicitly left the effort bound for owner judgment before investigation. It did not establish an executable budget, but preserved that as an unresolved decision rather than merely labeling the probe bounded.
- **Recovery after success:** preserved the mission, completed prerequisite and proposal-only hardening suggestions; the fresh lead requested the absent format/sample and parser details before detailing milestone 1. It did not reopen the probe or accept the hardening proposals.

- **Exhausted inconclusive setup:** also preserved the consumed two-attempt limit, kept the accelerator unverified rather than disproven, rejected infrastructure expansion and recommended milestone 1 with the ordinary parser.

These observations do not establish improvement over main.

## Review and checks

An independent Copilot Sol/high source review identified two wording problems: an unnecessary implication that a discovery brief must exist, and conflicting language about revising self-selected bounds. Both were verified against the guidance and corrected before candidate trials. The lead inspected the revised source and actual trial artifacts; independent review was not behavioral execution.

The existing evaluator's 25 infrastructure tests passed. No evaluator runtime or checker behavior changed. Those tests validate the harness, not Atlas adherence.

## Limits and disposition

The probe-bound miss and overcautious recovery recommendation remain visible. The draft strengthens the explicit contract but does not establish reliable enforcement, cost savings or causation for a previously reported incident. No additional trials were launched merely to obtain favorable outcomes. Setup failures and an interrupted mismatched baseline attempt remain in local evidence and are excluded from behavioral comparison. Public documentation omits raw traces and machine-specific details.
