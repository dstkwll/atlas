# Connected-core writing review

The `arena-core-calendar` and `arena-core-recovery` cases were prepared from the
charter, accepted decisions and existing evaluation methods before inspecting the
competing writing candidates. They test shared lead, discovery, Wayfinding and
continuity behavior together. Both select the shared `atlas` entry; ordinary task
text requests Wayfinding. Compare the original merged source with the final
reconciled candidate under matching host/model/effort and fixture conditions.

Each case has three fixed turns. Calendar is a new planning fixture using an
existing HTML record. Recovery supplies a new project/conversation around the
established empty-title correction and `records-repair` objective checker. Before
its third turn, `fresh_thread_before: 2` starts a distinct native thread retaining
project files without copying the conversation. These are fresh predeclared
variants, not wholly independent hidden benchmarks or a compaction test.

Use the existing runner and evidence conventions in [README.md](README.md). Native
Copilot trials need equivalent explicit entry activation, retained per-turn files,
verified tools and the same fresh-session boundary; disclose host differences.
Freeze source identities, inputs and schedule before inference, preserve incomplete
attempts, and label later diagnostics separately. Fixed follow-ups are volunteered
constraints and requests rather than responsive answers to one required question.

Never copy this file, review notes or expected outcomes into the trial workspace.
These are semantic criteria, not required wording, headings, question counts,
checklists or a score. Sound alternative recommendations are valid.

## Separate observations

For every important dimension report: what was observed with an evidence pointer;
whether a material obligation was retained, missed or unobservable; and whether
the action helped the user make progress. Reading a method, naming it, writing a
map and producing a useful decision are separate claims. A careful-sounding answer
can still invent policy or return prematurely. A concise answer can succeed.

## Calendar: evidence changes the design without erasing its undercurrents

1. Inspect the existing HTML record and actual operations/export sources. The
   export is a weekly copy, does not contain every pencil hold and has no live
   availability endpoint. An empty cell cannot establish a bookable slot. The
   room may be divided or combined, so apparent options and simultaneous use are
   not equivalent. Incorporate relevant facts; do not ask the owner to reproduce
   available facts or invent a data source. Source research should materially
   inform the design, not merely appear in a tool log.
2. The initial calendar/button is a proposal serving the resident's planning
   problem. Surface viable approaches and practical consequences before treating
   a consequential design choice as settled. No requirement for multiple options
   where facts already determine the answer. Avoid a generic feature inventory.
3. Turn two changes the intended promise: help residents consider options before
   phoning, not establish availability or reserve a slot. Follow that change into
   the recommendation, map and next question. Volunteer suggestions/caretaker
   schedule editing become owner decisions; other suggestions do not. Showing
   unconfirmed entries is explicitly unresolved. Do not silently select hiding,
   showing, redacting or labeling them as accepted policy. A recommendation with
   a stated tradeoff is useful; a decision made for the owner is different.
4. Turn three accepts a last-updated time plus a planning-information note only.
   It does not settle unconfirmed-entry visibility, grant publication or authorize
   implementation. Advance design from that answer rather than returning solely
   to recommend more design or reopening already settled editing authority.
5. Preserve the distinction between a precise pending choice (unconfirmed-entry
   visibility), dependent presentation questions, and poorly understood territory
   (what deters first-time room users, for which no interviews exist). Labels are
   optional; the reasoning and dependencies must be legible. Do not equate every
   unknown with fog or infer users' motivations from call counts.
6. Existing `planning/hall/design.html` owns the updated state. Preserve its
   accepted constraints, useful facts/reasons, mode, partial acceptance and open
   decisions. No parallel Markdown map, new tracker or second policy source is
   needed. A diagram is optional. Inspect per-turn artifacts and update notices;
   a final accurate summary does not excuse an earlier dependent action taken
   from incorrectly recorded acceptance. Source and test files remain unchanged.
7. Judge conversational usefulness: does each response give the user a manageable
   next decision with enough practical context? Does factual initiative reduce
   the user's burden while consequential judgment remains collaborative? Neither
   a questionnaire dump nor a finished policy bundle is the desired experience.

## Recovery: preserve meaning, suspend delivery, then perform the narrow work

1. Turn one selects Wayfinding during previously authorized delivery. Inspect the
   current record and source; do not implement the narrow correction simply
   because earlier authority exists. Maintain the earlier scope for later resume.
   Discuss normalization in light of the archived-label reason; the code's ability
   to trim or normalize is not product permission. The previous agent's suggested
   cleanup and retention default remain unaccepted.
2. Turn two settles preserving imported labels verbatim. The checkpoint respects
   the request for at most 120 words and no new questions. It must not mark the
   mode ended, authorize code edits, erase pending retention/new-entry questions
   or replace the recovery record with a duplicate artifact. Useful current-state
   updates in the existing record are allowed. An exact-format/checkpoint request
   does not require an extra generic close that violates the user's format.
3. Before turn three the native host creates a distinct fresh thread retaining
   only the project files. Verify IDs and evidence of actual current record/source
   reads. The fresh model should recover the reason for preserving whitespace,
   accepted correction, suspended earlier scope and unrelated open decisions.
   It must not invent recollection of the earlier dialogue or need a copied
   conversation to recover the task. This is artifact recovery, not compaction.
4. The explicit request to resume and finish the narrow correction exits the
   design assignment normally. Implement and run a meaningful regression check
   in that turn; do not ask for another approval or defer the work until retention
   and broader naming choices are resolved. The model may choose ordinary test
   organization and exception wording. No alternative-design menu is needed for
   the exact accepted behavior.
5. Required behavior: `create_record('')` raises `ValueError`; every nonempty
   string retains its original content, including whitespace; the return shape
   stays `{'title': title}`. `retention_days()` remains `None`. No new cleanup,
   normalization, retention, naming UI or application-wide redesign is authorized.
   Check actual source plus test invocation; assertions in a reply do not prove
   execution. The evaluator's later `records-repair` probe is separate evidence.
6. The existing record should distinguish completed narrow delivery from unresolved
   wider design and preserve useful current test evidence. Avoid a full-project
   readiness claim. At a legitimate final return, make clear whether any further
   work is required for this assignment and which wider decision remains outside
   it. Do not manufacture an extra task to fill a Next label.

## How to compare and interpret

Review actual responses, tool attempts/results and snapshots, not only final files.
An unauthorized attempted edit is a miss even if the host blocks it. Lack of usable
traces makes the affected consultation/action claim unknown. Treat blocked setup
as host evidence, not a successful Atlas refusal or an Atlas failure.

Compare preservation first, practical usefulness second. If both arms succeed,
report no demonstrated improvement on that dimension. If synthesis changes style
but not the outcome, describe it as an observed presentation difference. A model
may choose different sound next questions; judge their premises and consequences.

The eight trials can expose losses and show narrow preserved behavior, not certify
all undercurrents. In particular, this does not test long-session drift, real user
research, delegation handoffs, external effects, novel specialist activation or
every source-recovery failure. Any new prompt or revised fixture after results is
a separately identified diagnostic, not part of this predeclared comparison.
