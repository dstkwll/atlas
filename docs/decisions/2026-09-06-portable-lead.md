# Portable lead package

## Decision and rationale

Atlas is a standalone guide enacted by the host's main agent. One canonical skill at `plugins/atlas/skills/atlas/` owns its behavior; optional references provide deeper methods. Native entry points and useful explicit skills expose that guidance without a second policy source or required agent hierarchy.

This keeps the installation and mental model small while allowing substantial discovery, design, implementation and review. External tools and frameworks remain optional: their setup and coordination costs need a demonstrated benefit for the actual project. Runbook count is not an architectural limit; relevance governs consultation.

Accepted commitments remain distinct from provisional choices. Human authority, consequence-driven assurance, independent evidence, bounded correction and durable authority transitions constrain execution. Discovery and advisory work can be complete assignments. These boundaries apply without imposing fixed stages or mandatory reviewers on every edit.

A narrow deterministic mechanism can be considered when an objective failure and real consumer justify it, and guidance plus existing host or repository facilities cannot adequately prevent the failure. There is no authorized general controller, scheduler or universal workflow in the package.

## Boundaries and evidence

The portable lead receives authority from the adopting project's instructions and current assignment. It carries no maintenance, publication or historical permission from this repository. Product use and public maintenance must remain possible without personal coordination sources.

[Copilot packaging](2026-09-06-copilot-plugin.md) and [setup](../../SETUP.md) describe native entry points. [The charter](../architect-charter.md) preserves product intent; [validation records](../validation/portable-lead.md) distinguish observed behavior from broader unverified claims. Historical implementation and review evidence remains in Git and merged PRs.
