# Prepare a handoff

Use when another agent, session or team needs to continue a particular assignment. Tailor the handoff to the recipient's next task, not the full conversation. Preparing a handoff does not send it, execute its work or expand anyone's permissions.

Read current task state, accepted decisions and relevant live artifacts. Reuse the project's durable location and existing handoff convention; do not overwrite useful work or use a temporary-only location for a durable transfer unless requested. Keep the handoff outside the installed skill.

Include the minimum context needed to resume:
- Intended next outcome, current status and why the work is being transferred.
- Accepted commitments and relevant rationale; proposals, assumptions and unresolved judgment distinguished.
- Exact artifact/repository identity where it matters, relevant changes and evidence, current blockers and failed approaches worth avoiding.
- Recipient authority and prohibited effects, remaining explicit limits, dependencies and stopping/return conditions. Transfer does not replenish budgets.
- Next safe action and expected return evidence.
- Accessible sources with a short explanation of why each matters; relevant runbooks only when useful.

Use [Continuity and decisions](continuity-and-decisions.md) for authoritative state and [Deliver and repair](deliver-and-repair.md) for bounded worker briefs. For a planning handoff, link the PRD or tickets rather than duplicate them. Preserve enough binding context inline if the recipient cannot access a source; unresolved inaccessible authority must stay a blocker. Name where the recipient can load required guidance and verify availability when possible. Do not promise a host capability or skill that was not checked.

Protect environment boundaries. Do not move protected work data to a personal destination or treat redacting obvious secrets as sufficient export approval. If needed, prepare an approved local handoff or a safe abstract summary, clearly stating what the recipient cannot verify.

Review the packet from the recipient's perspective: can they recover intent, authority, relevant evidence and the next action without asking the user to replay the session? Contradictions and unknown execution effects must remain visible. State what was actually checked; a readable packet is not proof of successful transfer.

Return the handoff path and its intended use. Recommend the next step or perform an already-authorized transfer through the available host. Creating this artifact alone does not authorize sending messages, publishing, spawning work or executing tickets.
