# User research and feedback evidence

Use when planning or interpreting user research, synthesizing customer feedback, or making a product decision whose uncertainty depends on actual users' needs, understanding or behavior. This includes contradictory feedback and requests to validate an assumed audience or journey. Ordinary collaboration with the project owner remains [Discover and design](discover-and-design.md); a settled implementation or mechanical edit needs no study. Consult only the relevant part of this method.

## Connect evidence to a decision

State the decision, uncertainty and what evidence could change the recommendation. Check existing observations before proposing new collection. Separate the owner's accepted goals from claims about users, direct observations from interpretation, and user requests from proposed solutions. A fictional persona, model role-play or expert walkthrough may reveal a testable hypothesis; it supplies no interviews, demand estimate or observed task success.

Choose the least costly adequate method. Interviews can explore context and needs; observing a realistic task can reveal interaction barriers; usage data describes instrumented behavior with its coverage limits. A survey or randomized experiment answers different questions and needs an appropriate sampling or assignment design. No method, participant count or research cadence is universally required. Explain why the selected evidence addresses this decision and where it cannot.

## Prepare a useful study

Identify relevant participants by their actual task, experience and context, including accessibility or support needs that could alter the outcome. Name recruitment and selection limits; availability does not establish representativeness. Keep screening limited to what is relevant and permitted. Use approved consent, recording, storage and retention practices; a study plan does not authorize contacting participants, scheduling, collecting sensitive data or purchasing services.

Prepare realistic tasks without teaching the desired solution. Define what successful completion would look like and capture what participants attempted, where they needed help, and the resulting outcome. Separate moderator assistance from independent completion. Use neutral follow-ups to understand an observed action rather than coaching participants or asking them to confirm a preferred design. A prototype's omissions can change behavior; preserve that limitation when interpreting results.

For an actual randomized product experiment, establish the baseline, assignment unit, outcome and guardrails, meaningful effect, exposure window and analysis/stopping approach before interpreting results. Consider repeated observations, contamination between groups, missing events and relevant segments. Consult appropriate project statistical expertise for power and uncertainty; do not invent a sample-size guarantee or demand that experiments reach significance. An inconclusive or harmful result is valid evidence, not a reason to keep sampling until the preferred conclusion appears.

## Synthesize without manufacturing agreement

Keep a traceable path from a finding to its source, date, participant or event context and observation. Protect identities and raw material in their approved environment. Distinguish direct quotations, paraphrases and inferred themes; never fabricate quotations or participants to complete a template.

Deduplicate repeated tickets, forwarded comments and multiple channels for the same underlying observation. Preserve distinct experiences and disagreements rather than collapsing them into a vote. Report the relevant denominator when known, selection bias, missing groups and uncertainty. Frequency in a support inbox does not establish prevalence among all users; a rare event can still matter because its consequence is severe. Avoid false precision from scoring priorities with guessed reach or impact.

Compare explanations and proposed interventions against the evidence. Retain counterexamples that could change the decision. Explain which recommendation is supported, which remains a hypothesis, and what focused observation would resolve the remaining uncertainty. Preserve accepted product judgment separately from the researcher's interpretation; new findings do not silently change commitments.

Return a study plan or evidence synthesis suited to the assignment, with the decision it informs, source limitations, actionable implications and next evidence step. A plan is not a completed study, an expert critique is not usability validation, and proposed changes have no measured benefit until checked. Use [Product interface review](product-design-review.md) for design critique and [User journeys](user-journeys.md) for implemented interaction checks when those activities become relevant.
