# Compare independent alternatives (Arena)

Use when a consequential question has several credible approaches or the first proposal may be anchoring the design. This is an optional runbook for the lead, not a standalone skill, required design stage or fixed model panel. Ordinary comparison can remain within Discover and design without launching an Arena.

Before launching independent candidates, explain the decision to resolve, why diverse attempts could help, the candidate scope, expected artifact and bounded effort. Obtain the user's confirmation of that run unless their explicit request already specifies or accepts it. General permission to design, explore or keep working does not confirm an Arena. Do not silently replace missing confirmation with a self-imposed budget.

Set a concrete total worker/attempt limit and practical deadline before starting. Use cost limits only when spending is observable and enforcement is available; otherwise disclose that limitation and use observable work/time bounds. Respect stricter host/project limits, reserve effort for comparison and verification, and do not restart or add candidates just because the first results disappoint. A changed budget requires renewed agreement. If the host cannot supervise the proposed run within its bounds, narrow it with the user or offer a clearly labelled comparison by the lead.

Give each candidate the same decision context, accepted constraints, permitted capabilities and expected evidence, with an independent writable location when writing is authorized. Let candidates form ideas before showing them one another's answers. Independence does not require a named model family, cloud execution or a communication board. Use available approved host delegation; do not install coordination infrastructure. Missing independent contexts must be reported, not simulated by changing personas.

Choose task-specific comparison criteria before viewing results. Compare candidates against those criteria and their assumptions, including a simpler option when credible. Read all returned artifacts; record dropouts and incomplete coverage. Agreement is not proof and disagreement does not automatically mean the brief was wrong. A candidate can win outright; do not combine weaker pieces merely to use every output.

Synthesize only compatible ideas, then verify the resulting proposal or artifact rather than relying on evidence for its parents. Use [Independent review](independent-review.md) when the decision warrants it. Return alternatives, important tradeoffs, recommendation, evidence and uncertainty, plus actual completed versus missing work. Record material comparison reasoning in the existing design record. Recommendations do not accept product choices or authorize implementation/publication beyond the assignment.
