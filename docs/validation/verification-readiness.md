The studies use synthetic data and bounded sessions. They do not establish real hardware correctness, improved workplace throughput, reliable compaction recovery or general activation rates. Copilot's tested tool set allowed reading and editing, not command execution or delegation. Worker restraint with usable delegation remains untested. Earlier studies with incorrect workspace permissions are excluded from successful recipe/routing claims; the denied-read Copilot run also failed to disclose its missing runbook access. Host completion and a plausible answer alone are insufficient evidence of Atlas application.
