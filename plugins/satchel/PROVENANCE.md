# Satchel provenance

`claim-check` is an original synthesis. The entries below distinguish each skill’s origin from later contributions. Original licenses remain in each adapted skill folder so they accompany standalone copies.

## define-goal

Modified for Satchel from [OpenAI's define-goal](https://github.com/openai/skills/tree/b0401f07213a66414d84a65cb50c1d226f99485a/skills/.curated/define-goal): adds portable drafting, explicit native-creation intent and truthful outcome reporting; preserves an unfinished conflicting goal and offers separate goal-backed work only where supported. The PR-review example now verifies resolution through a paginated audit of review-thread state. The upstream UI metadata is unchanged. The [Apache 2.0 license](skills/define-goal/LICENSE.txt) is included.

## reorient

Reorient began as the project owner's original synthesis. The Satchel revision incorporates guidance from Lauren Tan's [Ketchup](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/978ca6e9c0e012047cd5058b8e829240336e704e/pstack/skills/ketchup/SKILL.md) for the recap window and pending decisions, including the [later pending-item clarification](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/8838150c6b4829ea3c01782631213776d86df14a/pstack/skills/ketchup/SKILL.md). It uses normal discovery and concise catch-up, with new advice only on request; no sibling skill or hard word limit is required. The original [MIT license](skills/reorient/LICENSE) is retained for the Ketchup contribution.

## teach-me

Adapted from Matt Pocock's [teach](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/productivity/teach). Modified for normal discovery and focused conversational learning with optional practice; automatic course workspaces and their format references are omitted. The original [MIT license](skills/teach-me/LICENSE) is included.

## to-questionnaire

Adapted from Matt Pocock's [to-questionnaire](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/productivity/to-questionnaire). Modified for normal discovery, supplied context, multiple recipients, provisional assumptions and drafting within the requested scope. The original [MIT license](skills/to-questionnaire/LICENSE) is included.

## unslop

Adapted from Lauren Tan's [unslop](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/8838150c6b4829ea3c01782631213776d86df14a/pstack/skills/unslop/SKILL.md). Modified for normal discovery and meaning-preserving edits; authorship claims, blanket style bans and sibling-skill rule identifiers are omitted. Later revisions explicitly cover the agent's own drafts and express ideas from Sushruth's [human-narrative](https://github.com/sushruth/human-narrative-skill/tree/d211f762201e0035b302fcf3c9d2f53b349606d3) skill in new prose: broader contrast examples, cold-read reference checks, private-label clarification and evidence-grounded hedge removal. The adaptation preserves meaningful contrasts, searchable identifiers, frequency and scope, and writer voice; fresh-reader assistance is optional. The original Lauren Tan [MIT license](skills/unslop/LICENSE) is retained; no human-narrative text is copied.

## wait-what

Adapted from Matt Pocock's [wait-what](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/productivity/wait-what). Modified for normal discovery, faithful explanation repair and optional glossary use. The original [MIT license](skills/wait-what/LICENSE) is included.

## why

Adapted from Lauren Tan's [why](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/8838150c6b4829ea3c01782631213776d86df14a/pstack/skills/why/SKILL.md) and its epistemics, investigation and source-playbook references. Modified for normal discovery and bounded decision research without required sibling skills, workers or integrations. The original [MIT license](skills/why/LICENSE) is included.

## writing-for-agents

Adapted from Matt Pocock's [writing-for-agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents), including its mechanics reference. Modified to preserve authority and exceptions, qualify model/host claims and compare behavior proportionately. The original [MIT license](skills/writing-for-agents/LICENSE) is included. Additional authoring ideas from [Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/best-practices) are expressed in new prose.
