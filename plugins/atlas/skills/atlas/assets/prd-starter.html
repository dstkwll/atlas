Cancel work before it starts.

Give a job owner a reliable way to withdraw queued work, while keeping cancellation and execution from both winning.

Topic: queue cancellation · Status: product intent settled; implementation readiness unverified.

Intent and boundaries

The owner may cancel an unstarted queued job. Running jobs cannot be cancelled. Existing status polling must report the durable outcome.

Accepted in the synthetic assignment: owner-only access, queued-only cancellation, one atomic winner between start and cancel, and visible cancellation status. No separate rationale was supplied.

Out of scope: stopping running work, deleting history, bulk actions, a new queue, and publishing or implementing this plan.

One decision at the persistence boundary

Proposed realization: both operations condition their transition on the same queued state. The persisted result determines what polling reports. Exact response codes and status spelling remain open.

What must be observable

ScenarioExpected outcome
Owner cancels queued workCancellation persists, polling reports it, and subsequent start cannot run the job.
Another user attempts cancellationNo cancellation; follow the established disclosure and denial contract.
Start and cancel overlapExactly one commits; the losing operation cannot overwrite the winner.
Job already runningCancellation is denied and running state remains intact.
