The adaptation draws on the skill's [epistemics](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/8838150c6b4829ea3c01782631213776d86df14a/pstack/skills/why/references/epistemics.md), investigator and synthesizer guidance, and source playbooks. It retains historical rationale, precise source attribution, explicit inference, contradictions, gaps and the distinction between mechanics and motivation.

Changes: extends the method to ordinary decisions as well as software; uses proportionate sources and a supported-answer stopping condition; distinguishes proposed, accepted and superseded choices, stated reasons from verified motives, and independent corroboration from repeated accounts. It replaces guessing a consequentially ambiguous target with a scoped clarification. Normal automatic discovery replaces the donor's explicit-only setting. It removes the `poteto-mode` dependency, the companion-skill instruction, model registry and model defaults, Cursor-specific APIs, mandatory investigator/synthesizer structure, seven-category search requirement and fixed report format. No connector setup, external analysis or mutation is authorized by invoking this skill.

The [adaptation account](https://github.com/dstkwll/atlas/blob/main/docs/satchel/adaptations.md) records the retained behavior and deliberate changes. No external source or sibling skill is needed to use Why.
