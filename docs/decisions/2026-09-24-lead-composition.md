# A concise lead that combines methods

Atlas remains one persistent conversational lead backed by a portable method library. The owner authorized simplifying the lead and clarifying how it combines relevant methods, while preserving the current experience and authority boundaries. This corrects guidance within the existing architecture; it does not promote the library into a public skill catalog or create a routing runtime.

## Decision and tradeoffs

Consolidate repeated explanations of authority, continuity, worker briefing, repair and return conditions in the shared lead. Keep the complete routing map and standing obligations in that entry point. Clarify that several methods can contribute to one decision, their implications must be reconciled against accepted constraints, and new evidence can change the selection before dependent work. Domain facts and commitments remain in authoritative project sources.

| Option | Benefit | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retain the current lead unchanged | Avoids preservation risk | Leaves repetition and implicit composition unchanged |
| Consolidate the lead and make composition explicit | Keeps one interface and one shared behavioral source; reduces duplication | Still relies on model judgment and context retention; needs outcome checks |
| Expose most methods as native skills or specialist agents | May offer host-specific discovery or execution benefits | Expands the surface, varies by host, and does not itself establish useful application or mission continuity |

The selected change is the middle option. Individual native skills remain possible when a concrete discovery, reuse or execution need justifies them; this revision does not evaluate or rule out that option. Reduced instruction length is a maintenance property, not evidence of better model behavior.

## Preservation map

| Behavior | Candidate home |
| --- | --- |
| One lead through ordinary follow-ups; unchanged host controls | Lead opening |
| Assignment and authority distinctions; advice can complete; visible consequential alternatives and delegated-choice exception | Understand the assignment |
| Entry and changing-activity selection; read before dependent action; unavailable-source disclosure; actual-use and worker notices | Choose and combine useful methods; unchanged routing map |
| Durable record before dependent work, persisted authority, parent mission and position, approximate direction-check cadence, bounded investigation and recovery from current sources | Keep the whole mission recoverable; unchanged continuity and delivery references |
| Bounded workers, explicit context transfer, candidate-specific verification, independent evidence, proportionate repair and persistent retry limits | Delegate, verify and repair proportionately; unchanged delivery and review references |
| Continue authorized work, legitimate return, plain-language input and standing Next close | Continue the work, then make the way forward clear; existing continuation decision |
| Arena confirmation and request-only Reflect | Routing map and adjacent reminder |

No standing obligation is intentionally withdrawn. Detailed procedures stay in their existing referenced runbooks. The new composition guidance explicitly prevents treating the union of applicable checklists as a required workflow or letting the last-read method redefine scope.

Independent review also clarified three boundaries: a method name alone does not require a worker, but required independence still needs a fresh context or authorized human reviewer; unfinished method-specific constraints need recoverable sources and implications, without a history of every consultation; and the warning about extra workflow machinery concerns procedure failures, not ordinary product schemas or validation.

## Evidence boundary

The [validation record](../validation/lead-composition.md) distinguishes source preservation from native task behavior. Method consultation, useful integration, authority compliance and recovery are separate observations. Small synthetic samples cannot establish reliable triggering, long-conversation retention, native-skill superiority or equivalent behavior in another host. Existing application misses remain relevant; this change does not erase them.

The routing map remains unchanged, so improved first discovery is not the theory of change for this patch. Selection, retention and application failures need different remedies. Before changing packaging, inspect representative failures and the actual host's loading/recovery behavior. Broader routing cues or stronger use reporting remain hypotheses to evaluate, not mandatory additions accepted by this revision.
